What we do

We work across all three of these worlds, and we specialize in making them work as one system.

AI

AI applications built around your documents, images, data and workflows.

  • LLMs, RAG and AI agents
  • Computer vision and motion analysis
  • Speech and voice control

Immersive

Interfaces that put people inside the information, from a headset to a wall of screens.

  • Mixed, augmented and virtual reality
  • Real-time 3D and digital twins
  • Multi-user and geospatial experiences

Enterprise

The services and integrations that connect your applications, workflows and data.

  • Back ends, APIs and databases
  • Cloud, containers and DevOps
  • Integration with your business systems

Where they meet is our specialty: a trainee in mixed reality coached by AI, with results recorded in your learning system. A 3D model of your site that answers questions in plain English from your live data. An automated pipeline to take your CAD/BIM data into robotics simulation.
